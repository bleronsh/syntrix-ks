01 / Practice

A quiet workshop for software
that has to actually work.

We are a small, deliberate team. We take fewer projects than we could so that the ones we take are done well — designed honestly, built with care, and handed over in a state we'd be willing to inherit ourselves.

Our clients tend to find us through other clients. They come with a system that needs to be built, replaced, untangled, or understood. We start by listening, then write down what we heard, then move slowly until we're sure — and quickly once we are.

03 / Approach

We work the way
we'd want to be worked with.

i.

Write it down first

Before code, a short document: what the system is for, who it serves, what it must not do. The page is cheap; the bug after launch isn't.

ii.

Build the boring parts well

Logging, backups, error states, the second-Tuesday-of-the-month edge case. Boring done well is what people quietly rely on for years.

iii.

Stay close, then leave clean

We work alongside your team, share what we learn, and hand over systems someone else can confidently inherit. No vendor lock, no theatre.

iv.

Say no more than yes

We turn down work that doesn't fit, scope features that don't earn their place, and tell you when the right answer is doing less than you asked for.
